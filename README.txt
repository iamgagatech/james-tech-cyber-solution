HOW TO UPLOAD TO cPANEL
-----------------------
1. Download the zip
2. Log into cPanel → File Manager
3. Go to public_html (or your domain folder)
4. Upload and extract all files
5. Make sure the folder structure is preserved

IMPORTANT SETTINGS
------------------
1. Open contact.php and change:
   $to = "support@aether365.com";
   to your real business email.

2. Replace all instances of "Aether365" and the demo
   phone/email with your real company details.

3. For production client portal, replace the simple
   demo authentication with a real database + hashed
   passwords (or connect to your existing PSA / ticket
   system).
