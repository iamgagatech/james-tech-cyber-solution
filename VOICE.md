# Nestshield voice print — reuse these rules for every new page, post and email

1. DO say what we actually do: "We check Windows Defender is on, current and set right." DON'T say "comprehensive threat protection solutions."
2. DO name the actor and the order: "We look, we tell you, you approve, we fix." DON'T write benefits with no actor ("saves time", "gives peace of mind").
3. DO use the client's words: "My laptop does a weird thing" is a fine sentence. DON'T translate it into "anomalous endpoint behaviour."
4. DO use only numbers we can prove: 20 years (owner-confirmed), 15-minute first call, $1,500–$4,500 packages, 46 reviews at 4.9/5. Need a new number? Get it from Tech James — never round up, never invent.
5. DON'T use: unlock, elevate, empower, seamlessly, cutting-edge, tailored to your needs, in today's digital world, "it's not just X — it's Y", or any adjective you can't back with an example on the same line.

Read-aloud test: if it sounds like a press release, rewrite it until it sounds like Tech James on a call.
