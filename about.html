About Aether365

Specialized Microsoft 365 support built for American businesses.

We live and breathe Microsoft 365

Aether365 was founded with a clear mission: deliver high-quality, no-nonsense Microsoft 365 and Office 365 support to companies across the United States.

Unlike generalist IT providers, we focus exclusively on the Microsoft cloud stack. This specialization allows us to stay deep on the latest features, security controls, and operational best practices so our clients don’t have to.
